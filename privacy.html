Motion Lab X

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

The short version

Motion Lab X collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no network requests from the app itself. Your training log, levels, benchmarks and photos never leave your device.

What is stored

Your training history, exercise levels, benchmark numbers, tendon check-ins, photos and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device (or the browser's local storage for the web version). Nothing is ever transmitted.

Photos

Optional side-on squat photos taken during benchmark weeks are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, never included in exports, and deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

The optional weekly check-in reminder is scheduled locally on your device by the operating system. No server is involved at any point.

Video links

Some exercise cards offer a Watch link that opens a video search in your browser or the YouTube app. Those searches happen outside the app, only when you tap, and are governed by the platform you open them in.

Your control

  • Export: Progress tab, Export. Your full history as plain JSON, yours to copy anywhere (photos are not part of exports).
  • Delete everything: remove the app from your home screen, or clear site data for the web version. Every byte goes with it.

Health disclaimer

Motion Lab X is a training tool, not medical advice. Stop training and seek help for pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's public repository.
